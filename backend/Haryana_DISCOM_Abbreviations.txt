1. HARYANA POWER SECTOR ORGANIZATIONS
====================================================================

BBMB
Full Form: Bhakra Beas Management Board
Category: Haryana Power Sector Organizations
Description: Inter-state body managing power and irrigation from Bhakra and Beas projects; supplies share of power to Haryana.
Example: Haryana draws hydel power allocation from BBMB.

CMD
Full Form: Chairman-cum-Managing Director
Category: Haryana Power Sector Organizations
Description: Apex executive head of a Haryana power utility such as UHBVN/DHBVN/HVPNL/HPGCL.
Example: The CMD, UHBVN reviewed AT&C loss performance.

DHBVN / DHBVNL
Full Form: Dakshin Haryana Bijli Vitran Nigam Limited
Category: Haryana Power Sector Organizations
Description: Distribution company responsible for power supply in southern Haryana districts (Gurugram, Faridabad, Rewari, Hisar circle areas etc.).
Example: DHBVN issued a revised billing schedule for Gurugram circle.

HERC
Full Form: Haryana Electricity Regulatory Commission
Category: Haryana Power Sector Organizations
Description: State regulator that determines tariff and issues regulations for Haryana's power sector under EA 2003.
Example: HERC approved the annual revenue requirement of DISCOMs.

HPGCL
Full Form: Haryana Power Generation Corporation Limited
Category: Haryana Power Sector Organizations
Description: State generation company operating thermal power plants that supply electricity to Haryana DISCOMs.
Example: HPGCL's Panipat plant contributes to the state's generation mix.

HPPC
Full Form: Haryana Power Purchase Centre
Category: Haryana Power Sector Organizations
Description: Nodal agency for scheduling and coordinating power purchase for both Haryana DISCOMs.
Example: HPPC finalized the power purchase agreement for the coming quarter.

HVPNL
Full Form: Haryana Vidyut Prasaran Nigam Limited
Category: Haryana Power Sector Organizations
Description: State Transmission Utility (STU) responsible for intra-state transmission network (66 kV and above) in Haryana.
Example: HVPNL commissioned a new 220 kV substation.

MD
Full Form: Managing Director
Category: Haryana Power Sector Organizations
Description: Executive head responsible for day-to-day operations of a Haryana power company.
Example: The MD, DHBVN chaired the monthly revenue review meeting.

UHBVN / UHBVNL
Full Form: Uttar Haryana Bijli Vitran Nigam Limited
Category: Haryana Power Sector Organizations
Description: Distribution company responsible for power supply in northern Haryana districts (Panchkula, Ambala, Karnal, Hisar city areas etc.).
Example: UHBVN launched a special OTS scheme for defaulting consumers.

2. ELECTRICITY ACT 2003
====================================================================

APTEL
Full Form: Appellate Tribunal for Electricity
Category: Electricity Act 2003
Description: Statutory body under Section 110 of EA 2003 that hears appeals against orders of CERC/SERCs.
Example: The DISCOM filed an appeal before APTEL against the HERC tariff order.

CEA
Full Form: Central Electricity Authority
Category: Electricity Act 2003
Description: Statutory body under Section 70 of EA 2003 advising on national power planning and technical standards.
Example: CEA notified grid connectivity standards followed by HVPNL.

CERC
Full Form: Central Electricity Regulatory Commission
Category: Electricity Act 2003
Description: National-level regulator under Section 76 of EA 2003 for inter-state transmission and tariff matters.
Example: CERC regulates the tariff for inter-state generating stations supplying Haryana.

CTU
Full Form: Central Transmission Utility
Category: Electricity Act 2003
Description: Central agency under Section 38 of EA 2003 responsible for inter-state transmission planning.
Example: CTU coordinates with HVPNL for inter-state power evacuation.

DISCOM
Full Form: Distribution Company
Category: Electricity Act 2003
Description: Licensee under Section 14 of EA 2003 responsible for distribution and retail supply of electricity in a specified area.
Example: UHBVN and DHBVN are the two DISCOMs operating in Haryana.

EA 2003
Full Form: Electricity Act, 2003
Category: Electricity Act 2003
Description: The central legislation that unbundled the power sector, introduced open access, and created SERC/CERC/APTEL.
Example: Tariff determination in Haryana is governed by provisions of EA 2003.

OA
Full Form: Open Access
Category: Electricity Act 2003
Description: Provision under Section 42 of EA 2003 allowing consumers above a specified load to procure power from a source other than the DISCOM.
Example: A large industrial consumer applied for OA to purchase power from an IPP.

SERC
Full Form: State Electricity Regulatory Commission
Category: Electricity Act 2003
Description: State-level regulator under Section 82 of EA 2003; HERC is Haryana's SERC.
Example: HERC, as the SERC for Haryana, notified the Supply Code.

STU
Full Form: State Transmission Utility
Category: Electricity Act 2003
Description: State agency under Section 39 of EA 2003 responsible for intra-state transmission; HVPNL is Haryana's STU.
Example: HVPNL functions as the STU for the State of Haryana.

3. HERC REGULATIONS
====================================================================

ARR
Full Form: Annual Revenue Requirement
Category: HERC Regulations
Description: Total revenue a DISCOM needs to recover its costs, filed annually with HERC for approval.
Example: DHBVN filed its ARR petition for FY 2026-27 with HERC.

MYT
Full Form: Multi Year Tariff
Category: HERC Regulations
Description: HERC framework fixing tariff-determining parameters for a control period (usually 3-5 years) instead of annually.
Example: The current MYT control period for Haryana DISCOMs runs till FY 2027.

RST
Full Form: Retail Supply Tariff
Category: HERC Regulations
Description: HERC order specifying category-wise tariff to be charged to end consumers.
Example: HERC issued the RST order effective from the new financial year.

SLA
Full Form: Standards of Performance / Service Level Agreement
Category: HERC Regulations
Description: HERC-notified standards specifying timelines for new connections, fault rectification, and compensation for non-compliance.
Example: HERC's SOP Regulations mandate compensation for delayed new connections.

SOP
Full Form: Standards of Performance Regulations
Category: HERC Regulations
Description: HERC regulation prescribing minimum service standards for DISCOMs, including penalties for default.
Example: A consumer claimed compensation under HERC's SOP Regulations for a delayed meter replacement.

TVAF
Full Form: True-up, Verification and Adjustment Framework
Category: HERC Regulations
Description: Mechanism used by HERC to true-up actual expenses/revenue against approved ARR of previous years.
Example: HERC carried out the true-up exercise for UHBVN's FY 2024-25 accounts.

4. TARIFF TERMINOLOGY
====================================================================

ABR
Full Form: Average Billing Rate
Category: Tariff Terminology
Description: Average revenue realized per unit of electricity billed, used in AT&C loss calculation.
Example: ABR is compared with ACS to compute the revenue gap.

ACS
Full Form: Average Cost of Supply
Category: Tariff Terminology
Description: Average cost incurred by a DISCOM to supply one unit of electricity, including power purchase and O&M costs.
Example: The gap between ACS and ABR indicates the DISCOM's revenue shortfall.

APPC
Full Form: Average Power Purchase Cost
Category: Tariff Terminology
Description: Average cost of purchasing one unit of power by the DISCOM across all its sources; used in tariff, power-cost and revenue-gap analysis.
Example: The APPC year-wise trend is reviewed while filing the ARR with HERC.

FAC
Full Form: Fuel Adjustment Charge
Category: Tariff Terminology
Description: Charge levied to pass through variation in fuel cost of generation to consumers (also called FSA in Haryana tariff orders).
Example: FAC was revised due to an increase in coal prices.

FC
Full Form: Fixed Charge
Category: Tariff Terminology
Description: Demand-based tariff component charged per kW/kVA of sanctioned/contract load, irrespective of actual consumption.
Example: An industrial consumer's bill includes both Fixed Charge and Energy Charge.

FSA
Full Form: Fuel and Power Purchase Cost Adjustment
Category: Tariff Terminology
Description: HERC-approved quarterly/monthly surcharge to recover variation in power purchase cost from the approved tariff order estimate.
Example: FSA of Rs. 0.35/unit was levied for the quarter.

MMC
Full Form: Minimum Monthly Charges
Category: Tariff Terminology
Description: Minimum amount billed to a consumer per month regardless of actual/lower consumption, as per tariff schedule.
Example: A consumer with zero consumption was still billed MMC.

PPAC
Full Form: Power Purchase Adjustment Charge
Category: Tariff Terminology
Description: Alternate term used for pass-through of power purchase cost variations to consumers.
Example: PPAC is reviewed quarterly based on actual purchase cost.

TOD
Full Form: Time of Day Tariff
Category: Tariff Terminology
Description: Tariff mechanism charging different rates for energy consumed during peak and off-peak hours.
Example: Industrial consumers pay a TOD surcharge during evening peak hours.

ToU
Full Form: Time of Use Tariff
Category: Tariff Terminology
Description: Similar to TOD; differentiated tariff structure based on time-blocks of consumption, increasingly used with smart meters.
Example: Smart meter data enables ToU billing for HT consumers.

5. CONSUMER CATEGORIES
====================================================================

AP
Full Form: Agricultural Pumpset
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Consumer category for irrigation tube-well/pumpset connections, typically metered/unmetered flat-rate supply.
Example: AP category consumers receive subsidized tariff for tube-well connections.

BPL
Full Form: Below Poverty Line
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Domestic consumer sub-category eligible for concessional tariff/subsidy under government schemes.
Example: BPL category consumers get a lower slab rate for the first block of units.

Bulk Supply
Full Form: Bulk Supply
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Supply given at a single point to a bulk consumer (e.g., a group-housing society, township or institution) that redistributes internally. Haryana connection/load reports list "Bulk" and "Bulk Supply (Domestic)" as separate columns.
Example: A group-housing society is billed as a single Bulk Supply consumer.

DMRC
Full Form: Delhi Metro Rail Corporation
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Dedicated bulk/traction consumer category for the metro rail network where its lines draw power from the DISCOM's area.
Example: DMRC appears as a distinct bulk consumer column in the connection/connected-load reports.

DS
Full Form: Domestic Supply
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Consumer category for residential/household electricity connections.
Example: DS category tariff is charged in a slab-wise structure.

EHT
Full Form: Extra High Tension (Consumer)
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Consumer supplied at extra-high voltage (typically 132 kV and above), used for very large industrial/traction loads.
Example: A large steel plant takes supply at EHT (132 kV).

FPO
Full Form: Farmer Producer Organisation
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Agro/agri-allied consumer category (grouped as "Agro Ind./FPO" in the reports) covering farmer producer organisations and agro-processing units.
Example: An "Agro Ind./FPO" connection covers the agro-processing load of a farmer producer organisation.

Gaushala
Full Form: Gaushala (Cow Shelter)
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Concessional consumer category for cow-shelter establishments, listed as a separate column in Haryana connection/load reports.
Example: Gaushalas are billed under a subsidised category as notified by the State.

HT
Full Form: High Tension (Consumer)
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Consumer supplied at 11 kV and above, typically large industrial/commercial loads.
Example: An HT industrial consumer is billed on a kVAh/TOD basis.

LT
Full Form: Low Tension (Consumer)
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Consumer supplied at 230V/440V, covering domestic, small commercial and small industrial loads.
Example: Most domestic connections in Haryana fall under the LT category.

NDS
Full Form: Non-Domestic Supply
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Tariff category for commercial/shop/office connections.
Example: A retail shop is billed under the NDS category.

NRS
Full Form: Non-Residential Supply
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Alternate/legacy term for commercial (non-domestic) consumer category.
Example: NRS category rates are higher than DS category rates.

PSP
Full Form: Public Street Lighting / Public Water Works and Sewerage Pumping
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Consumer category covering municipal street lighting and public water-supply/sewerage pumping loads.
Example: The municipal corporation is billed under the PSP category for streetlights.

PWW / P.W.W.
Full Form: Public Water Works
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Consumer category for public water-supply and sewerage pumping installations run by municipal/public bodies (a subset of the PSP category); listed as "P.W.W." in the connection reports.
Example: The town's water-works pumping station is billed under the P.W.W. category.

SP
Full Form: Small Power / Supply
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Category for small industrial/power loads distinct from bulk large industrial supply.
Example: A small workshop is connected under the SP category.

TW
Full Form: Tube-well
Category: Consumer Categories
Description: Sub-category of agricultural connection specifically for groundwater irrigation tube-wells.
Example: TW connections often use a flat-rate tariff based on pump HP.

6. BILLING & REVENUE
====================================================================

ACD
Full Form: Advance Consumption Deposit
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Deposit collected in advance against anticipated consumption, adjustable in future bills.
Example: A new bulk consumer paid ACD along with the security deposit.

Arrear
Full Form: Arrear (Outstanding Dues)
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Unpaid balance of a previous bill carried forward to the current bill, often attracting surcharge.
Example: The consumer's bill showed an arrear of Rs. 4,500 from last month.

FAC
Full Form: Fuel Adjustment Charge
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: See Tariff Terminology chapter; also appears as a distinct line item on the consumer bill.
Example: FAC is shown separately in the billed amount breakup.

Gross Billing
Full Form: Gross Billing
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Billing method where the full generated/exported energy (e.g., from rooftop solar) is billed separately from import, without netting.
Example: A solar consumer opting for gross billing is paid a separate feed-in tariff.

Interest on Security
Full Form: Interest on Security Deposit
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Annual interest payable/adjustable by DISCOM to consumer on the security deposit held, as per HERC norms.
Example: Interest on Security was credited in the consumer's March bill.

Late Payment Surcharge (LPSC)
Full Form: Late Payment Surcharge
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Additional charge levied when a bill is not paid within the due date.
Example: LPSC of 1.5% was added for the delayed payment.

MMC
Full Form: Minimum Monthly Charges
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: See Tariff Terminology chapter; the floor amount charged each billing cycle.
Example: MMC applied since actual bill was below the minimum threshold.

Net Billing
Full Form: Net Billing
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Billing method for net-metered consumers where import and export units are netted before billing.
Example: Under net billing, only the net consumed units are charged.

OTS
Full Form: One Time Settlement
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Scheme allowing defaulting consumers to clear outstanding dues within a limited window, usually with a waiver/rebate on surcharge or interest.
Example: UHBVN launched a special OTS scheme for defaulting consumers.

PDCO
Full Form: Permanent Disconnection Order
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Order for permanently disconnecting a consumer's supply, typically due to long-term non-payment or consumer request.
Example: PDCO was issued after six months of continuous non-payment.

Reconnection
Full Form: Reconnection (Charges)
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Restoration of supply after disconnection on payment of outstanding dues and reconnection fee.
Example: Reconnection was carried out within 24 hours of dues clearance.

Security Deposit
Full Form: Consumption Security Deposit
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Refundable deposit collected at the time of new connection, equivalent to estimated consumption for a fixed period.
Example: Security Deposit is revised periodically based on average consumption.

Sundry Assessment
Full Form: Sundry Assessment
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Ad-hoc billing raised for irregularities such as meter defect, theft, or unbilled periods, outside regular billing cycle.
Example: A Sundry Assessment bill was raised after the meter was found burnt.

TDCO
Full Form: Temporary Disconnection Order
Category: Billing & Revenue
Description: Order for temporarily disconnecting supply, usually for short-term non-payment, reversible on dues clearance.
Example: TDCO was issued for a consumer with two months' pending bill.

7. METERING
====================================================================

AMI
Full Form: Advanced Metering Infrastructure
Category: Metering
Description: End-to-end system of smart meters, communication network and data management enabling remote read/control.
Example: RDSS mandates AMI rollout for all consumers in Haryana.

AMR
Full Form: Automatic Meter Reading
Category: Metering
Description: Technology to remotely collect meter readings without manual visits, precursor to full AMI.
Example: HT consumers' meters were enabled with AMR for monthly billing.

Boundary Meter
Full Form: Boundary Meter
Category: Metering
Description: Meter installed at the interface point between two utilities/areas to account for energy exchange.
Example: A boundary meter records energy flow between DHBVN and UHBVN areas.

Check Meter
Full Form: Check Meter
Category: Metering
Description: Additional meter installed alongside the main meter for cross-verification of consumption.
Example: A check meter was installed at a large HT consumer's premises for audit purposes.

CT
Full Form: Current Transformer
Category: Metering
Description: Instrument transformer that steps down high current for accurate metering/protection.
Example: HT metering uses CT along with PT for accurate energy measurement.

CTPT
Full Form: Current Transformer - Potential Transformer (Metering Unit)
Category: Metering
Description: Combined CT-PT unit used for metering HT/EHT consumers where direct metering is not feasible.
Example: The CTPT ratio was verified during the meter testing.

DLMS
Full Form: Device Language Message Specification
Category: Metering
Description: International protocol/standard used for communication with smart/AMR meters.
Example: New smart meters comply with the DLMS/COSEM protocol.

Energy Meter
Full Form: Energy Meter
Category: Metering
Description: General term for a meter measuring electrical energy consumption in kWh/kVAh.
Example: A single-phase energy meter is installed for domestic connections.

HES
Full Form: Head End System
Category: Metering
Description: Central software system that communicates with smart meters to collect data and send commands.
Example: The HES receives daily consumption data from all AMI meters.

MDM
Full Form: Meter Data Management (System)
Category: Metering
Description: System that validates, stores and processes meter data received from HES for billing and analytics.
Example: MDM flags meters with abnormal consumption patterns.

MRI
Full Form: Meter Reading Instrument
Category: Metering
Description: Handheld device used by meter readers to capture and download meter readings.
Example: The meter reader used an MRI unit for spot billing.

Net Meter
Full Form: Net Meter
Category: Metering
Description: Bidirectional meter recording both import from and export to the grid, used for rooftop solar net-metering.
Example: A net meter was installed for the residential rooftop solar consumer.

Prepaid Meter
Full Form: Prepaid Meter
Category: Metering
Description: Meter requiring advance recharge; supply is disconnected automatically on balance exhaustion.
Example: Government offices are being shifted to prepaid meters under RDSS.

PT
Full Form: Potential Transformer (Voltage Transformer)
Category: Metering
Description: Instrument transformer that steps down high voltage to a standard low value for metering/protection; used together with a CT for HT/EHT metering.
Example: HT metering uses a CT along with a PT for accurate energy measurement.

Reference Meter
Full Form: Reference Meter
Category: Metering
Description: Highly accurate standard meter used to test and calibrate field meters.
Example: The testing lab uses a reference meter to check accuracy of field meters.

Smart Meter
Full Form: Smart Meter
Category: Metering
Description: Digital meter with two-way communication capability enabling remote reading, TOD billing and prepaid functionality.
Example: RDSS targets 100% smart metering across Haryana DISCOMs.

Tri-vector Meter
Full Form: Tri-vector Meter
Category: Metering
Description: Meter that simultaneously records kWh, kVAh and kVARh for comprehensive energy accounting of HT/EHT consumers.
Example: A tri-vector meter is mandatory for industrial HT connections.

8. DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM
====================================================================

AB Switch
Full Form: Air Break Switch
Category: Distribution System
Description: Manually operated switch used to isolate a line/feeder section in open air, without load-breaking capacity in some designs.
Example: The lineman opened the AB Switch before starting maintenance work.

Busbar
Full Form: Busbar
Category: Distribution System
Description: Conducting bar in a substation used to collect and distribute electrical power among incoming/outgoing feeders.
Example: The 33 kV busbar was extended to accommodate a new feeder bay.

Cable
Full Form: Underground Cable
Category: Distribution System
Description: Insulated conductor laid underground for power transmission/distribution, common in urban Haryana feeders.
Example: An 11 kV XLPE cable was laid for the new industrial feeder.

Capacitor Bank
Full Form: Capacitor Bank
Category: Distribution System
Description: Group of capacitors installed to improve power factor and reduce reactive power losses on the feeder/substation.
Example: A capacitor bank was installed at the 33/11 kV substation to improve voltage profile.

Conductor
Full Form: Conductor
Category: Distribution System
Description: Bare or insulated wire (ACSR, AAAC, etc.) used to carry electric current along overhead lines.
Example: Weasel/Dog/Panther conductors are commonly used on 11 kV/33 kV lines.

DO Fuse
Full Form: Drop-Out Fuse
Category: Distribution System
Description: Expulsion-type fuse used to protect distribution transformers, which "drops out" visibly on operation.
Example: The DO Fuse blew during the fault and was replaced during restoration.

DTR
Full Form: Distribution Transformer
Category: Distribution System
Description: Transformer stepping down 11 kV to 415V/230V for final consumer supply.
Example: A 100 kVA DTR was installed to serve the new colony.

Earth Wire
Full Form: Earth Wire / Ground Wire
Category: Distribution System
Description: Wire connected to earth to protect line/equipment from lightning and fault currents.
Example: The earth wire on the 33 kV line was checked during the audit.

Feeder
Full Form: Feeder
Category: Distribution System
Description: A distribution line (11 kV/33 kV) emanating from a substation supplying a defined area.
Example: The 11 kV rural feeder tripped due to a tree fault.

GO Switch
Full Form: Gang Operated Switch
Category: Distribution System
Description: Switch operated simultaneously on all three phases using a single operating handle/lever.
Example: A GO Switch was installed for load-break isolation on the feeder.

Insulator
Full Form: Insulator
Category: Distribution System
Description: Device (pin, disc, post type) used to electrically isolate the conductor from the supporting pole/tower.
Example: A cracked insulator caused a flashover on the 11 kV line.

kV
Full Form: Kilovolt
Category: Distribution System
Description: Unit of voltage (1000 volts); Haryana's network operates at 11 kV, 33 kV, 66 kV, 132 kV and 220 kV levels.
Example: The 132 kV grid substation feeds several 33 kV lines.

LA
Full Form: Lightning Arrester
Category: Distribution System
Description: Protective device that diverts lightning/surge overvoltages to earth, protecting transformers and equipment.
Example: LAs are mounted on both sides of the DTR for surge protection.

OCB
Full Form: Oil Circuit Breaker
Category: Distribution System
Description: Circuit breaker using oil as the arc-quenching medium, largely being replaced by VCBs in Haryana substations.
Example: The old OCB at the 33 kV bay was replaced with a VCB.

RMU
Full Form: Ring Main Unit
Category: Distribution System
Description: Compact SF6/air-insulated switchgear used in urban ring-main 11 kV distribution networks.
Example: An RMU was installed to improve reliability in the ring-fed urban feeder.

Stay Set
Full Form: Stay Set (Guy Wire Assembly)
Category: Distribution System
Description: Anchoring assembly (stay wire, rod, insulator) used to provide mechanical support to a pole against pull/tension.
Example: A stay set was fixed at the angle pole to prevent tilting.

VCB
Full Form: Vacuum Circuit Breaker
Category: Distribution System
Description: Circuit breaker using vacuum as the interrupting medium, widely used in 11 kV/33 kV switchgear.
Example: The feeder is protected by a VCB at the substation.

9. PROTECTION (ANSI RELAY NUMBERS)
====================================================================

21
Full Form: Distance (Impedance) Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay that measures impedance to determine fault location and distance on a transmission line.
Example: A 21 relay is used for distance protection on 132 kV lines.

25
Full Form: Synchronizing / Synchronism-Check Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay that checks voltage, frequency and phase match before permitting breaker closure to synchronize two sources.
Example: The 25 relay blocked closure due to phase angle mismatch.

27
Full Form: Undervoltage Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay that operates when voltage falls below a set threshold, protecting equipment from low-voltage conditions.
Example: The 27 relay tripped the feeder during a severe voltage sag.

32
Full Form: Directional Power Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay that detects reverse power flow direction, used to protect against reverse power conditions.
Example: A 32 relay prevents reverse power flow into a generator.

40
Full Form: Field (Loss of Excitation) Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay that detects loss of excitation/field current in a generator.
Example: The 40 relay protects the generator against loss of field.

46
Full Form: Negative Sequence / Phase Balance Current Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay that detects unbalance in phase currents, indicating faults like single-phasing.
Example: The 46 relay identified an unbalanced load condition on the feeder.

49
Full Form: Thermal (Machine/Transformer) Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay that protects equipment from overheating based on thermal image of load current.
Example: The 49 relay tripped the transformer on sustained overload.

50
Full Form: Instantaneous Overcurrent Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay that operates instantly when current exceeds a set threshold, without intentional time delay.
Example: The 50 relay cleared the close-in fault instantaneously.

50N
Full Form: Instantaneous Earth Fault Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Instantaneous relay operating on earth/ground fault current.
Example: The 50N relay tripped the breaker on a phase-to-earth fault.

51
Full Form: Time Overcurrent Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay operating on overcurrent with an inverse or definite time delay characteristic.
Example: The 51 relay provides backup overcurrent protection to the feeder.

51N
Full Form: Time-Delayed Earth Fault Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Time-delayed relay operating on earth fault current, coordinated with downstream protection.
Example: The 51N relay is set with a time delay to allow selective tripping.

59
Full Form: Overvoltage Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay that operates when voltage exceeds a preset threshold.
Example: The 59 relay tripped the capacitor bank on overvoltage.

64
Full Form: Ground (Earth) Fault Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay detecting earth faults, typically used for generator/transformer stator ground protection.
Example: The 64 relay detected a stator ground fault in the generator.

67
Full Form: Directional Overcurrent Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay that operates on overcurrent only in a specified direction of power/fault flow.
Example: The 67 relay ensures selective tripping in a ring-main network.

79
Full Form: Auto-Reclose Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay/function that automatically recloses a breaker after a trip to restore supply following transient faults.
Example: The 79 relay successfully auto-reclosed the feeder after a transient fault.

81
Full Form: Frequency Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay operating on abnormal system frequency (over/under-frequency), used for load shedding schemes.
Example: The 81 relay initiated under-frequency load shedding.

86
Full Form: Lockout Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Auxiliary relay that locks out the breaker after a trip until manually reset, preventing auto-reclosure.
Example: The 86 relay locked out the breaker following a major transformer fault.

87
Full Form: Differential Relay
Category: Protection
Description: Relay comparing currents entering and leaving a protected zone (transformer/busbar/line) to detect internal faults.
Example: The 87 relay tripped instantly on an internal transformer fault.

10. SCADA
====================================================================

ADMS
Full Form: Advanced Distribution Management System
Category: SCADA
Description: Integrated platform combining SCADA, DMS and OMS functions for real-time distribution network management.
Example: ADMS is being deployed under RDSS for Haryana's urban towns.

DMS
Full Form: Distribution Management System
Category: SCADA
Description: Software system for monitoring, control and optimization of the distribution network (load flow, switching, etc.).
Example: DMS helps operators plan feeder switching during maintenance.

FRTU
Full Form: Feeder Remote Terminal Unit
Category: SCADA
Description: RTU installed at feeder/ring main points to gather field data and execute remote control commands.
Example: FRTUs were installed on urban 11 kV feeders under the SCADA project.

HMI

Example: The Gurugram Circle covers multiple divisions of DHBVN.

Division
Full Form: Division Office
Category: Office Abbreviations
Description: Administrative unit headed by an Executive Engineer (XEN), comprising several Sub-Divisions.
Example: The consumer's complaint was escalated to the Division office.

HQ
Full Form: Head Quarters
Category: Office Abbreviations
Description: Corporate/central office of UHBVN or DHBVN responsible for policy and overall administration.
Example: The tariff petition was filed by DISCOM HQ before HERC.

JE
Full Form: Junior Engineer
Category: Office Abbreviations
Description: Field-level technical officer responsible for line maintenance and fault attendance in a Sub-Division.
Example: The JE attended the reported low-voltage complaint.

SDO
Full Form: Sub-Divisional Officer
Category: Office Abbreviations
Description: Officer (usually AE/AEE) in charge of a Sub-Division, the basic field operational unit.
Example: The new connection application was submitted at the SDO office.

SE
Full Form: Superintending Engineer
Category: Office Abbreviations
Description: Officer heading a Circle, overseeing multiple Divisions.
Example: The SE reviewed the Circle's AT&C loss performance.

Sub-Division
Full Form: Sub-Division Office
Category: Office Abbreviations
Description: Smallest administrative/operational unit headed by an SDO, handling field complaints and new connections.
Example: The Sub-Division office processes local consumer service requests.

XEN
Full Form: Executive Engineer
Category: Office Abbreviations
Description: Officer heading a Division, overseeing multiple Sub-Divisions.
Example: The XEN sanctioned the estimate for feeder augmentation work.

====================================================================
24. RIGHT TO SERVICE (RTS) & GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
====================================================================

RTS
Full Form: Right to Service (Haryana Right to Service Act, 2014)
Category: Right to Service (RTS)
Description: State law guaranteeing time-bound delivery of notified public services. DISCOM services (release of a new connection, shifting of meter, change of name, reduction of load, etc.) are notified under it, each with a Designated Officer, a time limit, and two levels of appeal.
Example: The release of a new connection is a notified service under the Right to Service Act.

Designated Officer
Full Form: Designated Officer (under the RTS Act)
Category: Right to Service (RTS)
Description: The officer responsible for delivering a particular notified service within its stipulated time limit. It varies by service — e.g., shifting of meter/service connection/line (LT) -> JE (In-charge); release of a new connection / change of name / reduction of load -> SDO (OP).
Example: The Designated Officer for shifting of an LT meter is the JE (In-charge).

FGRA
Full Form: First Grievance Redressal Authority
Category: Right to Service (RTS)
Description: The first appellate authority under the RTS notification, approached when the Designated Officer fails to deliver the service in time; for most DISCOM services this is the XEN (Executive Engineer).
Example: The First Grievance Redressal Authority for a delayed new connection is the XEN.

SGRA
Full Form: Second Grievance Redressal Authority
Category: Right to Service (RTS)
Description: The second/higher appellate authority under the RTS notification, approached against the decision of the First Grievance Redressal Authority (e.g., the SE / Superintending Engineer).
Example: If unsatisfied with the FGRA's decision, the applicant may appeal to the Second Grievance Redressal Authority.

Note: The RTS grievance channel (for delayed SERVICE delivery) is distinct from
the CGRF / Electricity Ombudsman channel (for BILLING and assessment disputes)
listed under "14. Vigilance & Enforcement".
