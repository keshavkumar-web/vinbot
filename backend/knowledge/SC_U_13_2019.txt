Subject:          Levy of fuel surcharge adjustment (FSA).

                  Please refer to S.C. No U-25/2018 dated 28.12.2018 in
                  accordance of which FSA, in terms of regulation 66 of the HERC MYT
                  regulations 2012, is presently being levied on various categories of the
                  consumers (except AP) @ 37 paisa per unit.

                  2. FSA for the third quarter of FY 2018-19 recoverable in the 4th
                  quarter of Financial Year 2018-19, has now been approved as per the
                  provisions of ibid Regulations and it has been decided that there will be no
                  increase in the FSA so as not to impose any additional financial burden on the
                  consumers. Accordingly, FSA @ 37 paisa per unit shall continue to be levied
                  on various categories of consumers, except AP, in the 4th quarter of FY 2018-
                  19 and onwards, till further orders.

                  The above instructions may be brought to the notice of all
                  concerned for careful and meticulous compliance with immediate effect.

                  Superintending Engineer/Commercial,
