Subject: Supply of meter, CT/PT and meter cubical and metering
equipment for release of connection.

It has been observed that the applicants applying for release of
connection especially are required to deposit CT/PTs to the subdivision office
for release of connection because of its non-availability in Nigam’s offices. In
order to have faceless, cashless and paper less delivery of services following
guidelines are hereby specified with respect to the arrangement of meter,
CT/PTs meter cubical and other metering equipments required for release of
connection:-

(a) The applicant while submitting the application will now have the only
option of Nigam meter. The system automatically calculates the charges
for Nigam Meter, CTs/PTs and Meter cubical, based on latest P&D
rates.
(b) The actual cost of meter, CT/PTs and metering cubical will be charged
by the Nigam, therefore no meter rental would be charged on this
account from the consumer.
(c) On the receipt of complete application, the concerned subdivision shall
take necessary action for the arrangement of above material from the
store required for release of connection.
(d) CE(MM) shall also undertake the annual procurement of CTs/PTs .
metering cubical and meters of various sizes in line with the
requirement of field offices.
(e) In case the material is not available in the store, the same will be
procured by concerned SE(OP) through DPC/CPC. Concerned SE(OP)
shall ensure that in line with the Nos. of connections applied in the area
the requisite inventory is always maintained. The cost under DPC/CPC
procurement should be within ± 10% of P&D rates notified every 6
months.
(f) In case the price variation is within ±10% of P&D rates, the same should
be adjusted in the first energy bill to be given to the consumer.

(g) In no case the consumer be asked to procure CTs, PTs meter and
metering cubical.
(h) In case any consumer voluntarily desired to supply his own meter, in
that case he should be given the technical specification, which is
available on the website of UHBVN and he should be directed to
procure the meter of that specification and to test the meter from any
M&T lab. Any applicable testing fees will be charged in first energy bill.
(i) CE(MM) henceforth shall stop the practice of empanelment of meters,
CTs/PTs and metering cubical.

The above instructions should be brought to the notice of all
concerned for strict and meticulous compliance.
