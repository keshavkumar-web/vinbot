Subject: Comprehensive Instructions and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
for Consumer Grievance Redressal, Implementation of CGRF /
Ombudsman / HERC Orders.

Hon’ble Haryana Electricity Regulatory Commission (HERC), vide Order
dated 24.04.2026 in Case No. HERC/P. No.25 of 2026 has directed to issue a Standard
Operating Procedure (SOP) for complying with the orders of CGRF / Electricity
Ombudsman / Commission / Court effectively.

This Sales Instruction contains a consolidated and comprehensive SOP, in
compliance with HERC (Forum and Ombudsman) Regulations, 2020 as amended, Sales
Circulars U-09/2022, U-20/2024.

1. Objective
To establish a uniform, transparent, consumer-friendly and regulation-
compliant framework for:
• Redressal of consumer grievances.
• Timely implementation of CGRF / Ombudsman / HERC orders.
• Proper defence of cases before judicial and quasi-judicial forums.
• Fixation of responsibility, disciplinary action and recovery of costs.
• Effective monitoring and review at all levels.

5. Digitized Workflow through UHBVN CGRF Platform
The entire grievance redressal workflow—including registration, routing,
hearings, order issuance, implementation, monitoring, and reporting—shall be executed
exclusively through the UHBVN CGRF IT Platform. The IT Wing shall ensure
uninterrupted availability, auto-routing, alerts, dashboards, MIS reporting and user
support. Manual processes or offline handling are strictly prohibited.

5.1 Responsibilities of IT Wing
The IT Wing shall ensure:
• Full operational readiness of the CGRF IT Platform;
• Auto-routing based on jurisdiction and monetary value;
• Secure digital hearing and order processes;
• Automatic alerts for timeline breaches;
• MIS dashboards for Divisional, Circle, Zonal and Corporate monitoring;
• Regular maintenance, upgrades, and uptime assurance;
• User training and helpdesk support.

6. Defending of Cases before CGRF, Ombudsman, HERC & Courts
All cases shall be defended diligently. The concerned CA/SDO/XEN/CBO
shall be responsible for facts, records, timely filing of replies, engagement of counsel
and presence on each date of hearing.

7. Compliance with CGRF/Regulatory/Court/ Tribunal Orders
All orders of Courts, Tribunals, CGRF, Ombudsman and HERC shall be
complied with promptly. No order shall be ignored, delayed or selectively implemented.

8. Erroneous / Disputed Billing Grievances
Grievances relating to Billing/Audit HM’s shall be handled as per Sales
Circular No. U-16/2022 and U-09/2024. However, once adjudicated by CGRF or
Ombudsman, implementation shall be strictly as per this SOP.

9. Penal Provisions & Disciplinary Action
Any dereliction of duty, delay, negligence or non-compliance shall attract
action under UHBVN Employees (Punishment & Appeal) Regulations, 2018. Penalties
may include censure, withholding of increments, recovery of loss, or major penalty
proceedings.

10. Recovery of Costs & Financial Loss
Any cost, compensation or penalty imposed by CGRF, Ombudsman, HERC
or Courts due to negligence or delay shall be recovered from the responsible
officer(s)/official(s) in addition to disciplinary action.

11. Review & Monitoring Mechanism of implementation of CGRF / Ombudsman
Orders
Weekly reviews at XEN level, fortnightly at SE level, monthly at CE level,
bi-monthly at Director/Operations level and quarterly reviews at WTDs level shall be
conducted.

12. Accountability & Reporting
Delays, non-compliance with Forum orders, or failure to use the IT
platform shall be recorded in the officer's performance appraisal and may attract
administrative action under UHBVN rules and HERC Regulations, including
proceedings under Section 142 of the Electricity Act, 2003.
This Sales Circular shall be read in conjunction with Sales Circular U-
09/2022, U-20/2024. All concerned shall ensure meticulous compliance with these
SOP guidelines.
The above instructions should be brought to notice of all concerned for
meticulous compliance.
