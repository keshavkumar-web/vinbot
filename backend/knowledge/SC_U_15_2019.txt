Subject:- Mhara Gaon Jag Mag Gaon - Amendment.
Please refer to sales circular no U-21/2015 dated
02.07.2015 vide which Mhara Gaon Jag Mag Gaon Scheme was
launched in UHBVN. The intent of the scheme was to curb power theft,
improve billing efficiency, quality of service to the consumers and to
allow increased supply hours to villages where there is decrease in
losses and improvement in collection efficiency is observed.

2. The paras at Sr. No. 5 and 6 of the aforementioned
circular stipulate as under:-
“5. For measuring the power supplied to the village the meters
shall be installed on all the points from which 11 KV lines
goes into the village. After allowing for the specified
quantum of technical losses below 20%, if the village pays
bills to the extent of 90% of power supplied, their electricity
supply will be increased from 18 to 21 hours.
6. After the defaulting amount of the feeder remains less than
10%, 24 Hours supply will be provided.”

3. It was however noticed that there were feeders which had
opted for MGJG scheme and where considerable progress had been
made in the implementation of the scheme. Although the AT&C losses
on these feeders had decreased considerably and collection efficiency
had also improved, yet the progress was not to the extent to entitle
these villages for 24 hours supply strictly as per the benchmarks set in
the said sales circular.

4. In order to promote MGJG scheme in the Nigam, it has
been decided to relax the conditions mentioned at Sr. No. 5 and 6 in
Sales circular U- 21/2015 to the extent that supply hours may be
increased on the RDS feeders where work under MGJG has been
completed and there is substantial reduction in T&D losses and an

increase in collection efficiency. The BODs in its meeting dated
26.03.2019, has granted ex-post facto approval to these amendments.
5. Other terms & conditions of Sales Circular No. U-21/2015
shall remain unchanged.

The above instructions may be brought to the notice of all
concerned for strict and meticulous compliance.
