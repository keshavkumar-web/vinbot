Subject:    Details of the GIS Application Module developed under R-APDRP
            /IPDS/IT project & SOP for regular updation of the GIS database thereof.

As you are aware that, under R-APDRP Part-A (IT) project implementation,
system of GIS had been developed along with the mapping of the electrical assets (Substations,
Feeders with HT lines, DT/Fs with LT lines and allied electrical accessories etc.) and indexing
of consumer w.r.t 18 designated R-APDRP towns covering 47 subdivisions of UHBVN.

2. Under Phase-II of IT project implementation, i.e. scaling up of the existing RAPDRP/IT system
in 17 IPDS towns covering 18 Sub-Divisions and in remaining Non-R-APDRP areas of UHBVN,
scope of GIS work was limited to GPS based asset survey and mapping of electrical assets
(Substations, Feeders with EHT/HT lines, DT/Fs and allied electrical accessories etc.) on the
GIS platform, without any consumer indexing.

3. Under the said Phase-II of IT project implementation, field survey work and digitization of
the asset data on the GIS platform has also been completed by the GIS agency (M/s RAMTeCH
Software Solution Pvt Ltd) engaged by UHBVN.

However, tagging of consumers on Feeders/DTs further is to be maintained in the GIS database
as per the details to be provided by the field offices/subdivisions.

4. With the advent of the GIS platform and considering the changes (addition/
deletion/augmentation), w.r.t assets and consumer, happening in the electrical system on
regular basis due to our field level operation activities, it was felt that capturing of these
changes (herein called as delta changes) and regular updation of the GIS database thereon shall
be very important as far as maintaining the sanctity of GIS system is concerned.

5. M/s RAMTeCH Software Solution Pvt Ltd, apart from the work of development of GIS
platform for the electrical assets in IPDS & Non R-APDRP areas of UHBVN, had also been
entrusted with the following activities under the contract award:

- GIS application upgrade along with advance facilities, functionalities & features i.e.
  Web Application facility, Mobile Application feature to facilitate Capturing of GIS
  delta change and viewing of information.

- GIS delta updation w.r.t complete area under UHBVN - This involves creation of a Back
  Office Team at Disaster Recovery Center, Panchkula and field teams with one no. GIS survey
  resource per Division for verification & validation of the delta change information to be
  provided by each subdivisions on regular basis.

7. Deliveries under the project awarded to M/s RAMTeCH Software Solution Pvt Ltd., have been
made by the firm and the task of GIS delta updation w.r.t complete area under UHBVN (R-
APDRP/IPDS/Non R-APDRP) has now been taken over by the agency.

8. Activities, which will attribute to GIS delta changes are as listed below:
   I. Release of New Connection (NC) & Modifications (EOL/ROL) in the existing
      connection
      a. Using Existing Asset
         i. Without Augmentation
         ii. With Augmentation
      b. Using New Asset
   II. Replacement of any Dead/Defective/Burnt Meter/Meter equipment.
   III. Any modification of existing electrical assets with replacement/augmentation/
        addition etc.
   IV. Revamping or Refurbishment of electrical system/network under any specific
        scheme or mission mode project.
   V. Feeder supply source change/reconfiguration (Permanent /Temporary nature).

9. Keeping in view the requirement of maintaining a duly updated GIS system along with the
establishment of GIS delta change agency (M/s RAMTeCH Software Solution Pvt Ltd) for
capturing of the system changes and regular updation of the GIS database w.r.t R-
APDRP/IPDS/Non R-APDRP areas under UHBVN, the following SOPs needs to be adhered to in
letter & spirit to ensure that the GIS database is kept updated & maintained for all times to
come with quality AM/CI data:
   I. Field officer/official/area in-charge (SDO/JE/AFM/AOR) shall download/install
      ESRI Survey123 App and Collector App on their Mobile and walk
      through/navigate it for their understanding of the SVR format and other features.
      This App has been got customized by R-APDRP wing for capturing the GIS delta
      change information.
   II. Field officer/official/area in-charge (SDO/JE/AFM/AOR) shall make use of this ESRI
       Survey123 for intimating the delta change information to Back Office RAMTeCH
       Team at DRC Panchkula.
   III. For the usage of this application, the user credentials shall be created by R APDRP
        wing. Necessary training for the usage of this App shall also be arranged by R-APDRP
        wing in collaboration with RAMTeCH Team.
   IV. Any delta change intimation, immediately after its occurrence, shall be
       captured/reported by the field officer/official/area in-charge (SDO/JE/AFM/AOR)
       only through ESRI Survey123 App and uploaded on the GIS system. It shall be the
       sole responsibility of officer/official/area in- charge that no delta changes in the
       system is left unreported.

V.  It shall be the sole responsibility of the field officer/official/area in-charge
(SDO/JE/AFM/AOR) to intimate correct information of delta changes in time (i.e.
immediately after the completion of the field task/work) for its updation in the GIS
database & application.

VI. Upon receipt of the delta change intimation from the field office, Back Office
RAMTeCH Team at DRC Panchkula shall assign the task to its field resource/team
posted at the Division office for the verification & validation of the change
intimation.

VII. After the field verification & validation of delta change intimation, a report will be
submitted by the field team member of RAMTeCH to its Back Office Team at DRC
Panchkula who after completing the data digitization process and ensuring quality
checks shall update the GIS database accordingly and publish the information
through the system of GIS for its onward visibility on the digital platform.

VIII. It shall be obligatory on part of the Field SDO/JE to reconcile the consumer data (For
IPDS towns) and assets on monthly basis in coordination with RAMTeCH resource
deployed at division level.

The above instructions brought to the notice of all concerned for careful and
meticulous compliance.
