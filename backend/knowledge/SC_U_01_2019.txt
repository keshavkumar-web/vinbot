Dated: 6.1.2019

Subject: Issue of demand notice for applicants seeking extension of
load on independent feeder under S.C. No.U-25/2017.

This is with reference to Sales Circular No.U-25/2017 dated
27.6.2017.

For the implementation of above sales circular, following
clarification is hereby given:-
(a) Wherever spare capacity is not available on independent feeder or
substation, in that case service connection charges are to be recovered
in demand notice irrespective of the date on which the consumer has
taken independent feeder connection.
(b) The service connection charges for extension of load on independent
feeder are not to be charged in case the applicant has taken
independent feeder connection before the date of notification of HERC
regulation i.e. 11th July, 2016 and provided further that spare capacity is
available in substation as well as feeder. However, while issuing the
demand notice of such consumer, the following notice be printed in the
demand notice:-
"This demand notice/service connection order is subject to the
outcome of review petition filed with HERC vide memo No.Ch-
13/SE/RA/N/F-56/Vol.XV dated 10.12.2018".
(c) Any independent feeder consumer who has taken independent feeder
connection after the notification of HERC regulation i.e. 11.07.2016 and
seeking extension of load are not to be benefited and service
connection charges are to be levied even if the spare capacity is
available in the substation and independent feeder.
(d) No refund be given to the consumer in view of the approval of U-
25/2017, as the petition its amendment has already been filed with the
Hon'ble Commission.

The above instructions may be brought to the notice of all
concerned for careful and meticulous compliance with immediate effect.
Sales Circular No. U-25/2017 is amended to the above extent.
