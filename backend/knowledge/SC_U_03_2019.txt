Subject: Issue of demand notice for applicants seeking extension of
load on independent feeder under S.C. No.U-25/2017.

This is with reference to Sales Circular No.U-25/2017 dated
27.6.2017 and Sales Circular No.U-01/2019 dated 04.1.2019. The matter has
been reviewed and the revised clarification are given as under:-

1. Wherever spare capacity is not available on independent feeder or
substation, in that case service connection charges are to be recovered in
demand notice irrespective of the date on which the consumer has taken
independent feeder connection.

2. For the applicants who have taken independent feeder connection before
the date of notification of HERC regulation i.e. 11th July, 2016 and provided
further that the spare capacity available in substation as well as feeder, the
payable service connection charges are to be calculated and intimated as
per following:-

a) The amount of estimate approved for providing original independent
feeder under self-execution or service connection charges/estimated
cost paid by applicant in department execution have to be checked for
finalizing the service connection charges payable for extension of load
on independent feeder. It should be ensured that only correct estimated
amount be considered based on correct size of cable/conductor etc.
commensurate with load as per instructions and excluding metering
charges, which are to be recovered as per the schedule of general
charges.

b) If the original correct estimated amount under self execution scheme or
service connection charges/estimated amount paid by the applicant also
covers the applicable service connection charges for extended load also
then no additional charges will be required to pay by the applicant
subject to the outcome of petition. However, while issuing the demand
notice of such consumer, the following notice be printed in the demand
notice:-

“This demand notice/service connection order is subject to the
outcome of review petition filed with HERC vide memo No.Ch-
13/SE/RA/N/F-56/Vol.XV dated 10.12.2018”.

(c) If the original correct estimated amount under self execution scheme or service connection charges/estimated amount paid by the applicant does not cover the service connection charges of extended load, then the applicant is required to pay service connection charges subject to outcome of the petition. However, while issuing the demand notice of such consumer, the following notice be printed in the demand notice:-

“This demand notice/service connection order is subject to the outcome of review petition filed with HERC vide memo No.Ch-13/SE/RA/N/F-56/Vol.XV dated 10.12.2018”.

(d) All existing applications at any stage are to be dealt as per the above guidelines to avoid any revenue loss to the Nigam.

3. Any independent feeder consumer who has taken independent feeder connection after the notification of HERC regulation i.e. 11.07.2016 and seeking extension of load are not to be benefited and service connection charges are to be levied even if the spare capacity is available in the substation and independent feeder.

4. No refund be given to the consumer in view of the approval of U-25/2017, as the petition its amendment has already been filed with the Hon’ble Commission.

Sales circular No. U-01/2019 stands superseded.

The above instructions should be brought to the notice of all concerned for careful and meticulous compliance with immediate effect.
