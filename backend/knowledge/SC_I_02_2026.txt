Subject: Issuance of Guidelines regarding streamlining of processes for
captive-status ascertainment, delegation of powers for OA
agreements, and issuance of login credentials to OA consumers.

The Nigam Management has approved the matter pertaining to the removal
of process impediments and implementation of a streamlined framework for:

1. Ascertainment of captive status of Open Access (OA) consumers and;
2. Delegation of authority to the Chief Engineer/System Operation, UHBVN,
Panchkula for execution of Open Access-related agreements.
3. Issuance of Login Credentials to Open Access Consumers.

In compliance with the said approval and adhering to the HERC Green
Energy Open Access Regulation 2023 & similar other Regulations, the following
guidelines are hereby issued for immediate implementation by all concerned:

1. Delegation of Powers for Execution of OA Agreements
The Chief Engineer/System Operation, UHBVN, Panchkula shall be the
authorised signatory for execution of:

o Medium-Term Open Access (MTOA) Agreements
o Long-Term Open Access (LTOA) Agreements
o Banking Agreements under HERC Green Energy Open Access Regulations
with prior approval of WTDs of the Nigam as per existing policy.

2. Streamlined Procedure for Ascertainment of Captive Status
The applicant shall submit requisite documents to SE/SO, UHBVN,
Panchkula for ascertaining captive status as per Electricity Rule 2005 or any
subsequent amendments/rules.

The following shall be treated as the minimum requisite documents for
issuance of captive status:

a) Application for captive status
b) HAREDA Registration / NOC
c) Undertaking to meet the 51% consumption requirement
d) Ownership Certificate (Format-1) as per CEA procedure
e) Auditor Certificate (Format-II) as per CEA procedure
f) Copy of recent energy bill

Based on the above documentation, the concerned SE/SO, UHBVN shall
check & ascertain Captive Status.

Further, the status of captive user shall be ascertained by CE/ SO, Chief
Financial Officer (CFO), FA & HPPC, Chief Engineer/ Commercial, Company Secretary
on yearly basis. This evaluation shall be based strictly on shareholding and energy
consumption in accordance with CEA and applicable HERC regulatory provisions.

If the captive user/drawl points fall under both DISCOMs, the applicant
shall obtain captive status certificates from both UHBVN and DHBVN.

3. Issuance of Login Credentials to OA Consumers

The OA consumer shall submit a request to SE/SO, UHBVN, Panchkula
for issuance of login credentials, along with supporting documents-grant of connectivity,
LTOA approval, registration of QCA, issuance of charging code, metering compliance
and COD declaration.

Upon basic confirmation of all the technical pre-requisites by CE/ SO &
Commercial, HVPN, Panchkula, the SE/SO UHBVN shall issue login credentials to the
consumer without seeking Management approval, even if the LTOA agreement is still
under execution.

Energy adjustment for captive consumption shall commence only after
execution of the LTOA agreement.

4. The above guidelines shall be read in conjunction with all the applicable
instructions of UHBVN and regulations of Hon'ble HERC issued from time to time and
shall be binding upon the stakeholders.

5. All concerned shall ensure strict compliance with these guidelines to
promote transparency, faster disposal, and improved service to Open Access consumers.

These instructions for streamlining the process of captive-status
ascertainment, delegation of powers for OA agreements, and issuance of login
credentials to OA consumers supersedes all earlier Office Orders/Instructions issued in
this regard.
