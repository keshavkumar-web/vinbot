Subject: Subsidized electricity tariff for Registered Gaushalas.

Please refer to Sales Circular No. U-05/2021 dated 18.03.2021 vide which
instructions regarding subsidized electricity tariff for Registered Gaushalas has been issued.
Now, in view of the CM Announcement Code No. 27860 regarding multiple electricity
connections to the Gaushalas having same registration number situated on different
locations, the updated instructions in this regard is as under: -

1. The State Govt. has decided that registered Gaushalas shall be given subsidized
   electricity supply @ Rs.2/- per unit with immediate effect and the remaining amount
   shall be claimed from the State Govt. as subsidy with following conditions:-
   i. Subsidy to be provided to the registered Gaushalas and
   ii. Subsidy to be provided on the actual consumption of electricity based on
       metered connection

2. Only Gaushalas registered with Haryana Gau Seva Aayog being registration authority
   shall be eligible for the subsidized tariff. The registration shall be verified from the
   Haryana Gau Seva Aayog through e-mail (hgsakpl@gmail.com) / letter by SDO 'OP'.

3. The necessary provisions in the billing software shall be made for depicting the
   subsidy amount of the said consumer in each billing cycle.

4. As per policy of the Nigam, all such connections shall be released on metered supply.

5. The electricity consumed for any purpose other than Gaushalas shall not be allowed
   at revised subsidized tariff.

6. The revised rates are exclusive of ED, M.Tax, Panchayat Tax and FPPAS.

7. The subsidized tariff bears the approval of Hon'ble Commission as intimated by
   Director/Tariff, HERC vide memo No.5085-86/HERC/Tariff/SV/2021 dated
   02.03.2021 and thereafter tariff orders issued by the commission time to time.

8. It is clarified that there are multiple Gaushalas with same registration number located
   at different premises which requires separate electricity connection on subsidised
   tariff. For such multiple Gaushalas with same registration number but situated on
   different locations, separate connections shall be provided by DISCOMs as required
   and authorised by the Haryana Gauseva Ayog. Haryana Gauseva Ayog will provide
   details of such Gaushala to the Haryana Discoms.

Sales Circular No. U-05/2021 is hereby superseded.
The above instructions should be brought to the notice of all concerned for
careful and meticulous compliance with immediate effect.
