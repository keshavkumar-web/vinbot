Subject: Mhara Gaon Jagmag Gaon for Dera / Dhanies on AP feeders
having PAT transformer.

Mhara Gaon Jagmag Gaon scheme was launched to curb theft
of power, improving billing efficiency and quality of service to consumers on
RDS feeders. Now, it has been decided to extend the said scheme to the
consumers residing in Dera / Dhanies connected on PAT enabled AP feeders.
The salient features of the scheme are as under: -

1. The principal amount of the old arrears, if any, will be recovered from
the consumers of the Dera / Dhanies in maximum 5 regular instalments
along with current bills. After payment of the last bill and arrear
instalment, entire surcharge amount will be waived off.
2. Reconnection at defaulter premises shall be allowed after payment of
first instalment without surcharge of the outstanding bill. Balance
outstanding amount will be paid in 4 instalments along with next current
bills.
3. The following load reduction activities shall be carried out before
extending relief to such consumers under the scheme: -
i) Replacement of bare conductor with Armoured cable.
ii) Replacement of defective/electro mechanical meters with healthy
electronic meters as a one time measure, the meters so replaced
would not be packed subject to checking from the M&T lab.
iii) Relocation of meters outside the premises on the pole.
iv) Proper metering of AP connections being fed from common DT in
the Dhani / Dera through electronic meter relocated on the pole.
v) Maintenance of LD system.
4. After the activities listed above are completed, the supply hours to the
Dera / Dhanies may be extended as deemed fit through PAT installed
on the AP feeder.
5. Connections to un-electrified Dhani / Dera existing on PAT enabled AP
feeders shall be released as per the existing instructions.

6. The energy auditing of common DTs (feeding AP as well as Domestic
consumers) falling on the PAT enabled AP feeders will be carried out
regularly and supply hours regulated accordingly.

The above instructions should be brought to the notice of all
concerned for careful and meticulous compliance with immediate effect.
