Subject: Instructions for preparation of self-execution estimates.

The existing instructions for preparation of estimates under self-execution contained in sales instructions No.U-02/2018 have been reviewed and following amendments are hereby made in the provisions contained in Sr. No.8 of the instructions:-
(a) For 33 KV works and feeder under self-execution, the estimate is to be approved by minimum SE (OP) level. Wherever the competency as per DOP lies with Chief Engineer (OP) or above, the same is to be approved by CE or other higher authority as per existing DOP.

(b) For 11 KV works and feeder under self execution, the estimate is to be approved by minimum concerned XEN (OP) level. Wherever the competency as per DOP lies with Superintending Engineer (OP) or above, the same is to be approved by SE or other higher authority as per existing DOP.

(c) For 33 KV and 11 KV lines, wherever length of cable/conductor required is more than 2 KM and is being supplied by applicant under self execution, the pre-dispatch inspections of cable/conductor is compulsory before dispatch to the site, so that the quality of material being utilized could be maintained as per the Nigam’s specifications. For the purpose concerned SE (OP) will depute the inspecting officers and will issue DA/DI after receipt of inspection report.

All other terms and conditions of sales instructions No. U-02/2018 remains unchanged.

The above instructions may be brought to the notice of all concerned for meticulous compliance.
