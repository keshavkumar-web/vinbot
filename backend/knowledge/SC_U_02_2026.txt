Subject: Standard Operating Procedure (SoP) for processing applications under
Saur Urja Protashan Yojana (SUPY).

The Government of India has launched the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli
Yojana on dated 13th Feb 2024 to promote the Rooftop solar installation across the
country. Accordingly, Government of Haryana has set a target of 2.22 Lakhs RTS
installation by 31st March, 2027. To expedite the progress of RTS installation in the
Haryana state a new structure for Financial Assistance i.e. Saur Urja Protashan Yojana
(SUPY) is approved by the State Government.

The Standard Operating Procedure (SoP) for processing applications under
Saur Urja Protashan Yojana (SUPY) describe the systematic workflow for applicants
seeking installation of Grid Connected Rooftop Solar (RTS) systems under the Saur Urja
Protashaan Yojana (SUPY), integrated with PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana (PMSG)
and ensures procedural uniformity, transparency, accountability, and clear ownership
among all stakeholders including applicants, DISCOM officials, empanelled vendors,
and supporting wings such as IT, Operations, FA/HQ and R-APDRP/Project Wing is as
detailed below: -

A. IMPLEMENTATION STEPS

1. Initiation of Application
The process commences when a consumer logs into the DISCOM portal.
At the time of login, a SUPY pop-up is displayed informing the consumer about the
availability of the scheme. The consumer may choose to proceed further by selecting the
option to check eligibility for availing financial assistance under SUPY.

2. Eligibility criteria
• Category-I (Non-Defaulter Consumers): Consumers who have remained non-
defaulters and have paid their energy bills before/on due date for the period
01.04.2022 to 31.03.2025, are eligible for availing financial assistance for
installation of RTS capacity upto 5 kW.
• Category-II (State Government Employees/Nigam/Board/Corporation etc):
Consumers who are State Government employees (HKRN employees only with job
security) with no default in the immediately preceding billing cycle, are eligible

for installing RTS capacity up to 5 kW. Entry of HRMS/HKRN ID along with
Consumer Account Number is mandatory for this category.

• Category-III (Antyodaya- SFA-Eligible Consumers):
  a. For family income in PPP upto 1.80 Lakhs
  b. For family income in PPP above 1.80 Lakhs and upto 3 Lakhs

Antyodaya consumers who are non-defaulters in the immediately
preceding billing cycle, eligible for State Financial Assistance (SFA), with RTS capacity
limited to 2 kW.

The eligibility validation will be carried out through DDO, billing database,
family ID and HRMS/HKRN data. The identified category and eligibility status are
displayed to the consumer and stored digitally for record and references.

3. Capacity Validation
Once eligibility is established, the system validates the maximum
permissible RTS capacity based on the identified category. Any attempt to select capacity
beyond the prescribed limits is automatically restricted by the portal.

4. Application on National Portal
After SUPY eligibility confirmation, the consumer proceeds to apply
independently on the National Portal. Upon successful submission, the consumer
receives a National Portal Reference Number.

The consumer is required to return to the DISCOM portal and enter the
National Portal Reference Number to link the two applications. This linkage is
mandatory for further processing under SUPY. Vendor Selection at this stage is locked
same as National Portal.

5. Execution and Upload of agreement for Financial Assistance under SUPY /
Undertaking
For consumers opting for financial assistance under SUPY, execution of
documentation as below is mandatory:

• For Category-I, Category-II and Category-III (b): Execution of agreement for
Financial Assistance under SUPY in DISCOM-prescribed format is mandatory
and is attached as (Annexure- ‘A’).

• For Category-II (State Government Employees/Nigam/Board/Corporation
etc): A written note/certification from the respective DDO of their
department ensuring State Government employees/(HKRN employees only with
job security) is also required to be submitted.

• For Category-III (a) & (b): - Indemnity bond will be submitted by consumer
(Annexure- ‘B’).

The executed document is uploaded on the DISCOM portal and verified by
the concerned field office (SDO).

6. Installation and Commissioning

Installation, inspection, testing and commissioning of the RTS system
shall be carried out strictly in accordance with the existing PM Surya Ghar SOP.
Empanelled vendors execute the installation, while verification and commissioning is
carried out by the SDO (Operations).

Upon completion, the Project Completion Report (PCR) along with
photographs and commissioning documents is uploaded on UHBVN portal.

7. Processing of Financial Assistance

Post-commissioning, the system computes the admissible financial
assistance under SUPY by considering:
• Benchmark installation cost
• Eligible Central Financial Assistance (CFA)
• Eligible State Financial Assistance (SFA), wherever applicable
• Consumer contribution
The balance amount represents the interest-free financial assistance to
be extended by the DISCOM.

8. CFA Disbursement by MNRE

Following approval of PCR, MNRE disburses CFA directly to the
consumer’s bank account as per its guidelines. This is independent of DISCOM-led
SUPY financial assistance. Subsequent to the disbursement of CFA, the concerned SDO
will update the CFA disbursement details on the DISCOM portal.

9. DDO approval for disbursement of Financial Assistance

After completion of CFA updation and bank detail verification, the
application is systematically forwarded to the DDO for processing of financial support
under the Saur Urja Protsahaan Yojana (SUPY).

The DDO examines the case for approval of financial assistance strictly as
per category-wise SUPY norms, as per following illustration:

10. Issuance of Financial assistance
Upon approval, the DDO shall arrange for disbursement of payment to the
consumer’s Bank Account after release of drawing limits from the Banking Section O/o
FA/Hqrs towards the interest-free financial assistance for Cat-I, Cat-II & Cat-III (b) and
advance SFA for Cat-III (a) & (b) consumers. The advance SFA will be paid by the State
Govt. in the same Financial Year and will be recouped in the revolving fund.
11. EMI Generation
Simultaneously with approval and disbursement of the financial
assistance, the EMI tenure (i.e. 24/12 electricity bills, as per prevailing billing either
monthly/bi-monthly) and EMI amount shall be shared by the SDO/OP with the R-
APDRP/Project Wing through the prescribed system or communication channel. Based
on these details, the RAPDRP/Project Wing shall map the EMI as an integral component
of the consumer’s electricity bill, ensuring that EMI recovery commences from the
subsequent billing cycle, in accordance with the scheme provisions and prevailing
regulations.
Completion of EMI mapping in the billing system and updation of transfer
details shall together mark the final closure of the application under SUPY, subject to
continued EMI recovery through the electricity billing mechanism.
In case of every first default i.e. payment of electricity bill of any such consumer who
has availed benefit of SUPY scheme, SDO ‘OP’ will ensure recovery/disconnection of
connection of the beneficiary as per MIS generated by R-APDRP/Project wing.

C. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF SUPPORTING WINGS FOR SUCCESSFUL IMPLEMENTATION

1. Role of Commercial Wing
i.  The Commercial Wing shall be responsible for the development, deployment and maintenance of all SUPY-specific functionalities on the DISCOM portal.
ii. This shall include, inter alia, display of SUPY pop-up at consumer login, system-driven eligibility determination, category identification, capacity validation, and locking of vendor selection strictly as per the MNRE/National Portal.
iii. Secure linkage with the MNRE/National Portal shall be ensured for verification of National Portal reference numbers and vendor details.
iv. The Commercial Wing shall also ensure proper workflow routing of applications across Operations, Finance & Accounts (F&A) Wing, and R-APDRP/Project Wing in accordance with the approved SOP.
v.  Automated SMS alerts to consumers at key stages, amount credited to consumer bank account shall also be ensured.
vi. Any portal-related or workflow issues impacting processing of applications under SUPY shall be promptly addressed by the Commercial Wing to avoid delays in implementation.

2. Role of R-APDRP/Project Wing
i.  The RAPDRP/Project wing shall provide the data base to verify default history of the applicants for the period 01.04.2022 to 31.03.2025 under Category-I for eligibility determination under SUPY.
ii. The R-APDRP / Project Wing shall also provide the database of the previous billing cycle on a monthly basis for the purpose of verifying the non-defaulting status of consumers under Category-II (State Government Employees) and Category-III (Antyodaya Consumers) for eligibility determination under SUPY.
iii. Upon receipt of the sanctioned financial assistance, EMI tenure (i.e., 24/12 electricity bills, as per prevailing billing either monthly/bi-monthly) and EMI details from the Finance & Accounts Wing, the R-APDRP/Project Wing shall ensure timely mapping of the EMI as an integral component of the consumer’s electricity bill, to be reflected from the subsequent applicable billing cycle, in accordance with approved terms and prevailing regulations.
iv. The R-APDRP/Project Wing shall ensure that recovery of EMI, delayed payment surcharge and non-payment consequences are governed strictly by the provisions applicable to recovery of electricity bills, without creation of any separate or parallel recovery mechanism.

v.  Periodic monitoring of EMI recovery status and generation of MIS/reports, as
required by the Commercial Wing, Operations or Finance & Accounts Wing, shall
also fall within the purview of the R-APDRP/Project Wing.
vi.  On completion of EMI tenure, a system generated of NOC/successful payment of
Financial Assistance will be provisioned.

3. Role of Finance & Accounts (F&A) Wing
i.   The FA/HQ shall be responsible for arrangement of funds and release of drawing
limits to all the accounting units under the Saur Urja Protsahan Yojana (SUPY).
ii.  The DDO in the field shall ensure that disbursement of Financial Assistance is
carried out in the approved manner and within the stipulated timelines under
the scheme.
iii. The financial assistance shall be drawn out of the total approved fund strictly as
per the requirements of the scheme, based on the applications processed and
approved under SUPY on monthly basis.
iv.  Upon due approval by the competent Drawing and Disbursing Officer (DDO), the
DDO wing in the field shall credit the approved financial assistance directly into
the applicant’s bank account through the prescribed electronic mode.
v.   All applications approved for financial assistance and received upto the end of a
month shall be cleared, and the corresponding amounts shall be credited to the
applicant’s bank accounts by the 10th day of the succeeding month.
vi.  Proper maintenance of financial records, reconciliation and documentation for
audit and compliance purposes shall be ensured by the Accounts Branch at field.
vii. Clear segregation between State Financial Assistance (SFA) and financial
assistance components shall be maintained by AO/Commercial at all stages of
financial processing under SUPY.
viii. The Finance & Accounts (F&A) Wing shall act as the nodal wing for
arrangement/allocation of financial assistance and SFA funds and DDO-wise
disbursement of financial assistance to consumers, and for accounting of
recovery of the financial assistance under the Saur Urja Protsahan Yojana
(SUPY).

4. Role of Operation Wing
i.   The Operations Wing shall act as the nodal field-level implementation wing for
the Saur Urja Protsahan Yojana (SUPY)
ii.  The Operations Wing shall verify the documents uploaded by consumers,
including agreement for Financial Assistance under SUPY, undertakings and, in
case of Category-II (State Government Employees), verification of the organisation
issued certificate/undertaking, wherever applicable.
iii. The Operations Wing, through the concerned Sub Divisional Officer (SDO), shall
be responsible for inspection, testing and commissioning of Rooftop Solar (RTS)
systems strictly in accordance with the PM Surya Ghar: Mufti Bijli Yojana SOP,
technical standards, and safety regulations.

iv. Post commissioning, the Operations Wing shall ensure timely verification and
approval of the Project Completion Report (PCR) and facilitate smooth progression
of the application to subsequent stages.

v. The Operations Wing shall coordinate with the Commercial Wing for portal-
related processing, with the Finance & Accounts (F&A) Wing for financial
approvals and disbursement, and with the RAPDRP/Project Wing for timely EMI
mapping (24/12 electricity bills as per prevailing billing either monthly/bi-
monthly) in the billing system.

vi. The Operations Wing shall be responsible for field-level consumer facilitation,
grievance resolution related to installation, inspection or commissioning, and
escalation of issues impacting timelines under SUPY.

vii. The Operations Wing shall monitor implementation progress at the field level and
provide periodic status updates and MIS to the Commercial Office, as required.

The above instructions should be brought to the notice of all concerned for
careful and meticulous compliance with immediate effect.
