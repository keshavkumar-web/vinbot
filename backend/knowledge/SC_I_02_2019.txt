Subject: Regarding supply of solar power on RESCO mode by
installation and commissioning of Grid Connected Rooftop
Solar Power Plants with Net metering facility in various
Jails and other Govt. buildings.

Kindly refer to Memo No. Ch-22/CE/HPPC/Solar/Prison
dated 03.04.2019 vide which it was decided to allow installation of RTSPPs
under RESCO mode at premises of 18 (10 belongs to UHBVN), Jails of the
Prison Deptt. for supply to respective Jails through Net Meter in terms of
Regulation 61 of the Haryana Electricity Regulatory Commission (Terms
and Conditions for Determination of Tariff from Renewable Energy
Sources, Renewable Purchase Obligation and Renewable Energy
Certificate) Regulations 2017 subject to any amendment in the relevant
HERC Regulations from time to time. As per the said Regulation, the
RESCO can supply/provide the power for the captive use of the premises
where the system is installed along with net meter and can sell the
remaining power to HPPC on the minimum last tariff discovered and
conveyed by HPPC as per the HERC Regulations. Regulation 61 of the
HERC RE Regulations, 2017 is reproduced below for ready reference:-
“The rooftop space available in the government organization,
institutions, buildings or vacant land of the same can also be provided
on lease/rent to the Independent Power Producer/ RESCO developer
for setting up of solar power projects.
For such sites the lease/rent rate shall be decided by a Committee of
Deputy Commissioner of concerned district, PWD (B&R) Department
and the Department owning the building. The developer can also
supply/provide the power for the captive use of the premises
where the system is installed along with net meter and can sell
the remaining power to HPPC on the minimum last tariff
discovered and conveyed by HPPC or to third party as per the
HERC Regulations.”

As intimated by CE/HPPC, the minimum last tariff discovered
through competitive bidding for procurement of 400 MW solar power by

HPPC through SECI, which has also been approved by HERC vide its
Order dated 27.02.2019, is Rs.2.44 per kWh.

Accordingly, any surplus power exported to the grid remaining
unadjusted under net metering shall be paid @ Rs.2.44 per kWh by way of
adjustment in the subsequent bills of the consumer by the concerned
XEN ‘OP’.

The above instructions may be brought to the notice of all
concerned for strict and meticulous compliance.
