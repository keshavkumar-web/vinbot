Subject: Schedule of tariff for supply of energy - Additional Surcharge for open
access consumers.

This is in reference to the Sales Circular No. U-11/2025 dated 26.08.2025
wherein additional surcharge @Rs.1.21/kWh for open access consumers was applicable
from 06.08.2025 to 29.04.2026.
2. Now, Hon’ble Commission, vide Order dated 30.04.2026 in Petition No. 05
of 2026 & 06 of 2026, has approved the revision of Additional Surcharge @ Rs.1.37/kWh
applicable to consumers availing power under Open Access mechanism effective from
30.04.2026 and shall remain in force until further revision by the Commission/Nigam.
3. Sales Circular No. U-11/2025 dated 26.08.2025 stands superseded, and
Sales Circular No. U-03/2026 dated 21.04.2026 stands amended to the above extent
only.

The above instructions may be brought to the notice of all concerned for
strict and meticulous compliance.
